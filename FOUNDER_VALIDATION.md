# Founder Validation Document

Status: Draft v0.1  
Owner: Founder  
Last updated: 2026-08-04

This document tracks evidence, not opinions. An assumption is not validated until an observable result and decision are recorded.

## 1. Working thesis

GrowFun currently tests whether parents value a short, repeatable way to guide a child's English sound-discrimination and pronunciation practice. AI may personalize the experience, but the product value must remain understandable without mentioning a model or framework.

Pronunciation and listening are now the first product hypothesis, but the detailed age range, buyer, effective activity progression, and willingness to pay are not yet confirmed. They remain validation questions rather than assumed facts.

## 2. North-star validation question

Will parents voluntarily complete a useful 10–15 minute GrowFun session with their child on multiple days per week?

## 3. Initial target and time box

- MVP build window: 6–8 weeks
- Pilot: 20–30 families
- Pilot duration: define before recruitment; provisional target is 4 weeks
- Primary behavior: repeated voluntary completion, not downloads or registrations

## 4. Assumption register

| ID | Assumption | Risk | Evidence required | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A1 | A specific group of parents has a recurring child-learning problem | Critical | 10+ interviews with repeated, recent examples | Unvalidated |
| A2 | A 10–15 minute guided session fits family routines | Critical | At least 60% of pilot families complete 3 sessions in one week | Unvalidated |
| A3 | Personalization improves the experience enough to matter | High | Controlled comparison or interview evidence tied to retention | Unvalidated |
| A4 | Parents trust the product's recommendations and controls | Critical | No unresolved safety concern in pilot; explicit trust feedback | Unvalidated |
| A5 | The child experiences the session as engaging rather than extra homework | High | Parent observation plus child-appropriate feedback | Unvalidated |
| A6 | Parents will pay or create another meaningful commitment | High | Price test, deposit, waitlist conversion, or renewal behavior | Unvalidated |

## 5. Questions that must be answered before feature expansion

1. Who is the first narrow user: child age, parent profile, language, geography, and learning context?
2. What happened recently that made the parent seek a solution?
3. What does the family do today, and why is it inadequate?
4. What is the single daily job GrowFun performs?
5. What outcome can a parent notice within one week?
6. What child data is truly necessary, and what must never be collected?
7. Who pays, how much, and what alternative receives that budget today?

## 6. MVP experiment sequence

### E1 — Problem interviews

- Recruit 12–15 parents matching one narrow hypothesis.
- Ask for concrete past behavior; do not pitch features first.
- Pass condition: at least 8 describe the same recurring problem and an active workaround.
- Decision: narrow the segment, change the problem, or proceed to concierge testing.

### E2 — Concierge daily journey

- Deliver the proposed 10–15 minute experience manually to 5 families for 5–7 days.
- Measure starts, completions, parent effort, child response, and next-day return.
- Pass condition: at least 3 families complete on 3 separate days without repeated reminders.

### E3 — Instrumented MVP pilot

- Recruit 20–30 families.
- Measure activation, completed sessions per family, week-one retention, parent effort, safety incidents, and qualitative value.
- Provisional success threshold: 60% activate, 40% complete 3 sessions in week one, and no unresolved high-severity safety issue.
- Thresholds must be finalized before the pilot to prevent post-hoc interpretation.

### E4 — Commitment test

- Test a clearly stated price or equivalent commitment.
- Pass condition must be set before exposure and segmented by user type.

## 7. Measurement dictionary

- **Activated family:** completes onboarding and one full daily session.
- **Completed session:** reaches the defined outcome event, not merely opening the app.
- **Week-one retained:** completes a session on a later day within days 4–7.
- **Parent effort:** self-reported minutes plus observable steps required outside the session.
- **Safety incident:** output or workflow that violates the product's child-safety policy or parent expectations.

## 9. Stop and pivot conditions

- Interviews do not converge on a recurring problem after two well-defined segments.
- Families require persistent reminders or the daily journey regularly exceeds 15 minutes.
- Personalization adds complexity without measurable behavioral or qualitative value.
- The product cannot meet defined child-safety and parent-control requirements.
